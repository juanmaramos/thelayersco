The grammar, under pressure.

Five compositions test convergence, review, integration, exception handling, and measurement.

B / Review

03 / Brand narratives

Make the operating change visible.

Six commercial stories test comparison, provenance, validation, scope, engagement rhythm, and consistency across the two initial practices.

F / Before and after

Fragmented work becomes one governed flow

The current state shows repeated handoffs and review. The target state keeps the same inputs but gives them one controlled route to an approved output.

G / Provenance

Evidence remains attached to the output

Source fragments pass through a codified method and remain visible at review.

H / Quality gate

Weak assumptions do not reach production

One path passes, one routes to expert review, and one stops with an explicit terminal.

I / Bounded scope

Improve one workflow without replacing the stack

Existing systems remain outside the implementation boundary. Only the selected workflow, controls, and approved write-back sit inside it.

J / Engagement model

Baseline. Validate. Implement. Improve.

Four stages rise as uncertainty reduces. Measurement returns to validation as a controlled input, not as an autonomous self-improvement claim.

K / Two practices

One operating model, applied twice

People and Workforce and professional-services delivery use the same workflow grammar. Only the source material and approved output change.

04 / Shape vocabulary

More range. The same discipline.

Five construction families extend the system through exact repetition, modular clustering, ordered depth, sparse activity, and nested control planes.

L / Versioned stack

Knowledge accumulates without losing structure

One slab is translated by one fixed depth step. The top plane remains the focus.

M / Operating cell

Specialist capabilities form one accountable team

Equal volumes share one field; position and one human marker establish ownership.

N / Ordered fan

Repeated work becomes an inspectable queue

Every panel is one primitive translated by the same 36 × −20 step. Stroke hierarchy, not improvised perspective, establishes front and back.

O / Signal field

Activity moves; the operating model stays still

A sparse dot field may pulse between states. The structural plane never drifts.

P / Nested control plane

The workflow sits inside the enterprise context

Concentric projected boundaries separate surrounding systems from owned scope.

05 / Landing applications

One claim. One visual verb.

Three page-level compositions translate the proposition, proof model, and two practices without introducing generic AI imagery or invented product interfaces.

Q / Judgment core

Keep experts at the point of judgment

Documents, structured intake, and codified preparation surround one accountable human decision. Only the approved result leaves on the primary path.

R / Evidence spine

Every decision can be traced back to its evidence

Selected fragments remain individually addressable while the evidence package moves through the method, review, and approved output. Missing evidence stops explicitly.

S / Practice diptych

Policy to decision. Method to deliverable.

The two practices share control and review patterns, but their recognizable source material and operational outputs remain specific.

06 / Operational ASCII

Machine-readable detail. Human-readable control.

Monospace notation records provenance and state without turning the interface into a terminal. It supports the SVG system; it never replaces the workflow explanation.

T / Provenance readout

One compact trace for source, rule, exception, and output

Columns align deliberately. Blue marks an applied or approved state; ochre underlines the accountable human review.
